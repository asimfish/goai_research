RENDERING TIERS (obey strictly — this is the figure's information hierarchy):
- TIER 1 macro group: a large rounded region with a very light tint, a 1 px border in the group's hue and a small bold group label in the top-left corner. Groups are containers only; they carry no content of their own.
- TIER 2 primary module: a white card with a 6 px top accent bar in the group's hue, a bold dark title, and ONE small line-art glyph left of the title. Only true modules are cards. Cards within a group share one width and one height.
- TIER 3 internal mechanism: INSIDE each module card, its steps are drawn as a compact horizontal mini-chain of small rounded tokens joined by short 1.5 px arrows — not as a bulleted list of sentences. A module that only names itself is a failure; the reader must see input → operation → output inside the card.
- TIER 4 labels: every variable, quantity, record item, criterion or caveat rides on a connector, a port or a small tag. None of them is ever its own box.
Nothing may appear at a tier above its role: a record item, a metric, a caveat or a question is never a module card.
