\section{Boxed Model-Assisted Precursor Diagnostic}

General LLZO preparation literature provides context for why precursor identity and processing history matter \cite{raju2021crystal,heywood2023tailoring}; it does \emph{not} validate the ranked sets below. The box reports a software-adapter diagnostic from the bundled raw outputs, not a chemical conclusion.

The diagnostic has one legitimate manuscript function: it makes the boundary between a verified software return and an evidence-qualified synthesis claim visible. It contributes neither chemical novelty nor an actionable route. The current entity warnings and absent conditions are failure signals to preserve, not fields to repair by inference.
