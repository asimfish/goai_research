# Scope and boundaries

## Working title

LLZO garnet solid electrolytes: synthesis, densification, and processing–structure–ionic-conductivity relationships

## Delivery language

English. The manuscript will use the English survey template and reader-facing scientific terminology.

## Time window

Primary evidence: 2020–present, with seminal pre-2020 LLZO papers retained only when needed to define compositions, mechanisms, or benchmark processing routes. Searches should prioritize the recent window while backfilling foundational studies.

## Included material systems

- Cubic and tetragonal Li7La3Zr2O12 (LLZO) garnets, including Al-, Ta-, Nb-, W-, Ga-, and related aliovalent substitutions when the paper reports synthesis, densification, structure, or conductivity.
- Composite/gradient LLZO bodies only when the LLZO phase and its processing–property contribution are separable.
- Processing additives, sacrificial powders, sintering aids, atmosphere control, and Li-loss compensation strategies.

## MECE subtopics

1. **Composition and phase stability:** cubic/tetragonal selection, dopant site occupancy, Li ordering, secondary phases, and phase-diagram/thermodynamic constraints.
2. **Powder and precursor synthesis:** solid-state, sol–gel/Péchini, co-precipitation, combustion, hydrothermal, mechanochemical, and scalable powder processing; precursor chemistry and calcination.
3. **Green-body forming and densification:** pressing, tape casting, cold/isostatic pressing, hot pressing, SPS/FAST, microwave and field-assisted routes; shrinkage, porosity closure, and Li volatilization.
4. **Microstructure and interfaces:** grain size, grain-boundary chemistry, wetting films, pores/cracks, electrode/electrolyte interfaces, and impurity segregation.
5. **Structure–transport relationships:** crystallography, defect chemistry, Li-vacancy concentration/order, tortuosity, and how bulk, grain-boundary, and total conductivity respond.
6. **Measurement and comparability:** impedance protocols, electrode configuration, density normalization, activation energy, humidity/atmosphere effects, and reporting standards.
7. **Processing–property design rules and scale-up:** integrated process windows, reproducibility, thick/large-area bodies, compatibility with co-processing, and unresolved bottlenecks.
8. **Near-neighbor and analogous systems:** garnet analogues (e.g., Li5La3Ta2O12), LLZO-related oxides, and the user-provided Ba–Y–Zn–Si–O phase-control studies as methodological analogues for phase purity/metastability (not as LLZO evidence).
9. **Phase diagrams and thermodynamic data:** Li–La–Zr–O and doped LLZO phase relations, Li2O activity/volatility, additive–LLZO reactions, and metastability evidence.

## Exclusions

- Sulfide, halide, polymer, and NASICON electrolytes as primary subjects (comparators may appear in one short context paragraph).
- Claims based only on nominal composition without phase/microstructure/conductivity evidence.
- Proprietary industrial recipes lacking enough processing and characterization detail to assess reproducibility.
- New executable laboratory protocols; the review reports published conditions with safety and evidence qualifiers.

## Core review questions

- Which precursor and forming choices most reliably produce phase-pure, high-density LLZO?
- How do dopants, additives, atmosphere, and thermal profile jointly control grain-boundary resistance and Li retention?
- Which structure and processing descriptors best predict total ionic conductivity across studies, and where are comparisons invalid?
- What minimum reporting set is needed for reproducible scale-up?
