\section*{Data Sources and Literature Coverage}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Data Sources and Literature Coverage}
The screened bibliography for this review contains 513 distinct records, retrieved from arXiv, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, Crossref, and DBLP with citation snowballing, deduplicated by DOI, and verified against publisher metadata (title, author list and order, year, venue). The complete record set, together with the per-entry verification verdicts, is provided in the accompanying evidence package (\texttt{evidence/papers.jsonl} and \texttt{run/CITATION\_AUDIT.md}). Only the records that support a specific statement are cited in the text and listed in the reference section; Table~\ref{tab:corpus-roles} summarizes how the screened corpus distributes across the scientific roles used in the comparison. A record's presence in the screened corpus does not by itself establish a phase in the Ba--Y--Zn--Si--O quaternary.
