\section{Challenges on the Path to Scalable Solar Fuels}
\label{sec:challenges}

Following the style of the strongest outlooks, we phrase each challenge as
a decision question with a measurement and a failure criterion rather than
as a wish list~\cite{sun2025design,doremus2024photocatalytic}.

\textbf{Can the field converge on portable metrics?} The comparison
problem of Section~\ref{sec:performance} is structural: rates without
wavelength-resolved AQY, donor identity, and normalization basis cannot be
aggregated~\cite{zhong2023oligo,wang2026bandgap}. The decision question is
whether journals and reviews require AQY($\lambda$) and STH -- with
light-intensity linearity checks inherited from oxide photocatalysis
~\cite{tang2008mechanism} -- as submission defaults. Failure looks like
another decade of leaderboards built on incommensurable
mmol\,g$^{-1}$\,h$^{-1}$ values~\cite{aslam2026a,gu2023recent}.

\textbf{Does activity survive the donor?} Nearly all Table~\ref{tab:single}
entries consume sacrificial electron donors. Overall water splitting on
COF platforms exists -- sp$^2$c frameworks at AQE 2.53\%
~\cite{cheng2023fully}, pore-confined Pt nanosheets at STH 0.23\%
~\cite{yang2023engineering}, bifunctional Pt/COF systems evolving
stoichiometric H$_2$/O$_2$~\cite{zhang2026bifunctional}, and
computationally paired HER/OER COF couples~\cite{wan2020a} -- but
efficiencies trail donor-assisted numbers by two orders of magnitude. The
measurable milestone is STH $\geq 1\%$ on an all-organic or COF-dominant
system with verified stoichiometry; persistent reliance on TEOA-class
donors within techno-economic models is the failure mode
~\cite{chen2026covalent,jia2026rational}.

\textbf{Is long-duration stability real?} Hour-scale retention with
post-mortem PXRD/XPS is becoming standard~\cite{he2023integrated,
lu2024copper}, but solar-fuel service means thousands of hours under
flow, oxygen crossover, and protonation drift
(Section~\ref{sec:protonation}). The decision experiment is a
1000-hour continuous run with periodic structural audit -- the COF
analogue of module aging tests; crystallinity--stability trade-offs
~\cite{ran2024progress,qin2024structural} and linkage hydrolysis under
operando acidity are the leading failure hypotheses.

\textbf{Can synthesis leave the sealed tube?} Gram-scale, days-long
solvothermal batches cannot supply reactor arrays. Flow synthesis of
TpPa-1~\cite{xu2026structural}, mechanochemical batches
~\cite{asokan2026structure}, microwave composite growth
~\cite{gao2025microwave}, and emulsion-interface morphology control
~\cite{ma2024rapid} define the candidate manufacturing set; the milestone
is kilogram-scale production retaining Section~\ref{sec:synthesis} order
parameters at $<$10\% penalty~\cite{wu2025recent,khalil2024two}.

\textbf{Can noble metals be engineered out?} The Pt-dependence spectrum of
Section~\ref{sec:performance} is trending in the right direction --
single-atom economy~\cite{dong2021platinum,tang2025engineering}, earth-
abundant single atoms~\cite{wang2024single,osemba2026synergistic,
younas2024a}, molecular Co catalysts~\cite{gottschling2020rational,
huang2025enantioselective}, and genuinely cocatalyst-free platforms
~\cite{ma2025engineering,zhang2025dual,zhao2025cocatalyst,yan2023metal} --
but matched-protocol parity with 3~wt\% Pt baselines remains the
unambiguous target~\cite{qu2024covalent}.

\textbf{Do photons reach the framework in a reactor?} Photoelectrode and
panel formats change the photon economics that powder assays hide: COF
photocathodes sustain tens of $\mu$A\,cm$^{-2}$~\cite{jia2025copper,
lu2024copper}, oxide-scaffolded junctions manage light and charge jointly
~\cite{niu2026fluorine,hao2025covalent}, and PEC-oriented reviews now
treat 2D COFs as absorber layers in their own right
~\cite{guntermann2026recent,peng2025sp2}. Reactor-level milestones --
areal STH under natural irradiance, seawater tolerance
~\cite{yu2025topology,zhong2023oligo,zhang2025dual} -- should replace
mass-normalized records as the field's headline numbers
~\cite{zhou2023recent,niu2023review}.
