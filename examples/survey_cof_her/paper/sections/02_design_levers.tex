\subsection{Four linkage families, four positions on the
stability--conjugation frontier}
\label{sec:linkages}

The linkage is the single decision that most strongly constrains everything
downstream, because it fixes both the degree of $\pi$-delocalization across
the framework and the hydrolytic stability that photocatalysis in water
demands~\cite{guntermann2026recent,qi2023recent,romero2021light,
mishra2023impact}; linkage-first taxonomies now organize COF photocatalysis
across reactions well beyond HER~\cite{yang2024covalent}.
Table~\ref{tab:linkages} summarizes the four families that dominate HER
work.

$\beta$-Ketoenamine (Tp-based) COFs, formed from
1,3,5-triformylphloroglucinol (Tp) and diamines through combined Schiff-base
condensation and irreversible enol-to-keto tautomerization, trade some
in-plane conjugation for exceptional acid and water stability, which is why
this family -- with TpPa-1 as its workhorse -- accounts for the largest share
of reported COF photocatalysts and composites~\cite{xue2023research,
wang2023review,sujatha20251}. The keto-locked backbone also supports
excited-state intramolecular proton transfer (ESIPT), whose induced
photoisomerization must be considered when assigning activity differences
within the family~\cite{weng2023effect}. Imine (C=N) COFs retain reversible
linkage formation and therefore high crystallinity, and their lone-pair-rich
linkages are protonation-responsive, a property Section~\ref{sec:protonation}
develops into a full design lever~\cite{chen2024hydrogen,yang2021protonated,
luo2024asymmetric}. Vinylene and sp$^2$-carbon frameworks extend conjugation
through C=C bonds that cannot hydrolyze, combining full $\pi$-delocalization
with chemical robustness: sp$^2$-carbon COFs manage the complete
photon-to-charge event sequence within one lattice~\cite{jin20192d}, survive
9~M HCl and 9~M NaOH~\cite{chen2019designed}, and, in a matched comparison,
an sp$^2$-carbon-linked COF catalyzed overall water splitting with an AQE of
2.53\% at 420~nm where its imine-linked analogue was inactive
~\cite{cheng2023fully}. Kagome-lattice sp$^2$c-COFs templated from an
imine precursor reach 105.3 and 80.0~mmol\,g$^{-1}$\,h$^{-1}$ in 3~wt\%
saltwater and natural seawater, respectively~\cite{yu2025topology}, and
pyrene-based sp$^2$c frameworks outperform their imine counterparts for the
same topology~\cite{hao2024a,lu2024copper}. Extending the acetylenic logic,
a graphdiyne-like diacetylene-rich linker delivers
10.16~mmol\,g$^{-1}$\,h$^{-1}$ against 3.71 and 1.13 for its non-GDY
analogues~\cite{lin2024diacetylene}. Aldol, Knoevenagel, and
Horner--Wadsworth--Emmons condensations now give synthetic access across
this family~\cite{peng2025sp2}. Finally, triazine-based frameworks
contribute nitrogen-rich acceptor cores with planar, fully conjugated
attributes shared with g-C$_3$N$_4$ and CTFs~\cite{hao20212d}; base- and
temperature-controlled routes now deliver crystalline triazine-sp$^2$c
hybrids~\cite{zhang2023design}, and the natural affinity of triazine cores
for g-C$_3$N$_4$ interfaces carries over to junction chemistry beyond HER
~\cite{qi2024triazine}.

Within each family, linkage-level editing tunes activity further: cyano
versus unsubstituted vinylene isomers show distinct structure--activity
relationships~\cite{nagar2025isomeric}, single-atom skeletal editing opens
irreversible Katritzky-type routes to acridinium frameworks
~\cite{pan2025rational}, and a matched three-linkage platform
(imine, enamine, and alkene on the same knot--linker pair) isolates the
linkage contribution directly, with the alkene member reaching an AQE of 6.7\% at
420~nm~\cite{mo2020alkene}. The emerging design rule is that conjugation
and hydrolytic robustness need not be traded off -- vinylene chemistry
achieves both -- but crystallinity cost and synthetic accessibility still
favor imine and $\beta$-ketoenamine platforms for systematic studies
~\cite{peng2025sp2,wu2025recent}.

\subsection{Donor--acceptor backbone engineering}
\label{sec:da}

The controlled variable in donor--acceptor (D--A) design is the intramolecular
dipole field, and its expected consequence is twofold: a narrowed optical gap
and a driving force that pre-separates the Frenkel exciton along the
donor-to-acceptor axis~\cite{liu2024dual,liu2025donor,shen2023efficient}.
Computation now leads synthesis here: density-functional screening of
triazine- and benzene-cored acceptor series shows exciton binding energy
($E_\mathrm{b}$) varies systematically with donor strength, and the
synthesized activity order follows the calculated $E_\mathrm{b}$ ranking
~\cite{qian2023computation}. The benchmark experimental results are
consistent: a $\beta$-ketene--cyano D--A pair prolongs carrier lifetime
dramatically and delivers an AQE of 82.6\% at 450~nm with Pt
~\cite{li2022covalent}; a pyrene-donor/dual-benzothiadiazole-acceptor COF
averages 12.7~mmol\,g$^{-1}$\,h$^{-1}$ under visible light, among the
highest for D--A-type systems~\cite{liu2024dual}; and trithiophene-based
D--A frameworks operate cocatalyst-free~\cite{naqvi2026design}.

Three refinements sharpen the basic rule. First, symmetry matters as much
as strength: highly symmetric D--A architectures cancel their dipoles and
weaken the built-in electric field, so breaking symmetry -- excitonic
dipole reorientation~\cite{bao2025excitonic}, asymmetric imine-linker
orientation (raising solar-to-hydrogen efficiency from 2.76\% to 4.24\%
~\cite{luo2024asymmetric}), or ground-state charge-transfer bonding that
directly intensifies the built-in field~\cite{shen2025ground} -- recovers
the driving force. Multistep charge transfer in single-crystalline COFs
shows how far directional field design can be pushed~\cite{jin2026abnormal}.
Second, the linker length and core conjugation co-tune the gap: biphenyl
linkers outperform benzene and triphenyl analogues in a matched isoreticular
series~\cite{dong2025synergistic}, progressive core $\pi$-extension promotes
exciton dissociation~\cite{li2026progressive}, benzobisoxazole-bridged
fully conjugated frameworks widen absorption while promoting dissociation
~\cite{liu2025fully}, fluorenone acceptor cores pair efficient exciton
dissociation with a well-defined active center~\cite{hao2023fluorenone}, and even 3D COFs, long excluded by their sp$^3$ nodes,
now sustain intrinsic metal-free photocatalysis~\cite{wang2024molecular}.
Third, heteroatom decoration acts as a local-field lever: fluorination
raises surface area, narrows the gap, and improves separation
~\cite{liu2021a}, with positional isomerism of fluorine alone moving AQY to
4.09\% at 500~nm~\cite{zhu2025fluorination}; chlorine placement creates
halogen-adjacent carbon HER sites active in seawater without cocatalyst
~\cite{ma2025engineering}; and push--pull side groups correlate directly
with ESIPT-mediated activity across matched six-membered substituent series
~\cite{huang2024regulating,jiang2024significant}. Local polarization
engineering in truxenone frameworks~\cite{hao2024regulating}, dipole
modulation of vinylene COFs~\cite{wang2024dipole}, and nitrogen-position
tuning that reverses framework polarity~\cite{haase2017structure} all act on
the same underlying variable. The design rule: maximize the net (not
nominal) D--A dipole per unit cell, verify with $E_\mathrm{b}$ or built-in
field measurements, and only then attribute activity gains to the D--A
lever~\cite{qian2023computation,bao2025excitonic}.

\subsection{Linkage protonation and acid engineering}
\label{sec:protonation}

Protonation is the cheapest band-structure intervention available: exposing
imine or azine lone pairs to Br{\o}nsted acids red-shifts absorption,
stabilizes the excited state, and switches on exciton dissociation channels.
The landmark demonstration lifted D--A imine COFs to
20.7~mmol\,g$^{-1}$\,h$^{-1}$ upon linkage protonation~\cite{yang2021protonated};
HCl-protonated COF-920 subsequently reached 364~mmol\,g$^{-1}$\,h$^{-1}$
under UV--visible light~\cite{dong2025synergistic,dong2025synergisticx}.
The lever composes with others: simultaneous protonation and conjugation
enhancement multiplies to an 88.4-fold gain over the parent framework
~\cite{he2024double}, surface protonation plus interfacial engineering
yields 18.23~mmol\,g$^{-1}$\,h$^{-1}$ without any cocatalyst
~\cite{duan2024protonated}, and protonation raises conductivity and
accelerates H$_2$ generation at Ru sites in vinylene frameworks
~\cite{yu2024vinyl}. The boundary condition is stability: protonation
equilibria drift under operation, so durability claims require post-reaction
structural verification (Section~\ref{sec:challenges}).

\subsection{Defect and vacancy engineering}
\label{sec:defects}

Beyond backbone substitution, deliberately installed defects create charge
funnels: amide-functionalized defective CTFs reach
17.6~mmol\,g$^{-1}$\,h$^{-1}$, a 6.3-fold gain over the pristine framework,
while preserving structural integrity~\cite{gu2026single}, and
missing-linker defects in porphyrin D--A COFs improve hydrogen evolution
alongside oxygen reduction~\cite{yang2026missing}. Crystallinity-resolved
studies caution that defect and order effects are entangled: molecular
engineering of tunable-crystallinity series shows activity tracking order
parameters rather than nominal composition~\cite{xiao2024molecular}, which
is why Section~\ref{sec:synthesis} treats crystallinity control as a
first-class synthesis variable rather than a nuisance.
