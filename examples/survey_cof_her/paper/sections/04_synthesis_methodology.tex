\section{Synthesis Methodology and Crystallinity Control}
\label{sec:synthesis}

Every claim in Sections~\ref{sec:levers} and \ref{sec:mechanism} is gated by
synthesis: crystallinity, stacking order, and morphology are set in the
reactor, and they co-determine absorption, exciton transport, and site
accessibility~\cite{qin2024structural,ran2024progress}. This section
organizes the methodology by decreasing thermodynamic control and increasing
practicality.

\subsection{Solvothermal standards and crystallization modulation}

Most COF photocatalysts are still grown solvothermally -- sealed-tube,
100--120~$^\circ$C, days-long reactions whose solvent pair, catalyst, and
water activity control the error-correction equilibrium
~\cite{wu2025recent,bagheri2020towards}. The Tp toolbox illustrates the
maturity of this control: 1,3,5-triformylphloroglucinol reliably delivers
crystalline, stable $\beta$-ketoenamine frameworks across many diamines,
which is a major reason Tp-COFs dominate the composite literature
~\cite{sujatha20251,xue2023research,wang2023review}. For imine frameworks,
cocrystal engineering of the monomer with p-toluenesulphonic acid regulates
crystal structure formation and enables hydrogen-bond-directed growth
~\cite{chen2024hydrogen}, while base identity and loading tune triazine-
sp$^2$c condensation~\cite{zhang2023design}. Alkalinity screening in
sp$^2$c photocathode synthesis shows the payoff quantitatively: optimal
conditions (110~$^\circ$C, 30~$\mu$L pyridine, 2~days) yield photocurrents
2.5$\times$ higher than suboptimally crystallized batches of the same
composition~\cite{jia2025copper} -- a reminder that inter-laboratory rate
disagreements often reduce to crystallinity differences
~\cite{xiao2024molecular,ghosh2020identification}.

\subsection{Fast, mild, and scalable alternatives}

The scale-up path runs through routes that abandon the sealed tube.
Mechanochemical grinding delivers $\beta$-ketoenamine COFs at room
temperature with moderate crystallinity and retained chemical stability,
and mechanochemically synthesized Tp-COFs now support systematic
structure--property studies in photocatalytic HER~\cite{asokan2026structure}.
Flow synthesis of TpPa-1 guided by green-chemistry principles modulates
structure continuously and points toward manufacturing-compatible
production~\cite{xu2026structural}. Microwave heating compresses
heterojunction growth to minutes~\cite{gao2025microwave,gao2025microwavex,
zhang2026bifunctional}, and nanoconfined synthesis at surfactant-stabilized
oil--water emulsion interfaces reconciles high reaction rate with morphology
control for imine 2D COFs~\cite{ma2024rapid}. Post-synthetic and
irreversible-bond strategies -- Katritzky-type skeletal editing among them --
expand the accessible linkage space beyond dynamic covalent chemistry
~\cite{pan2025rational}. Room-temperature and template-free routes remain
the least developed but most scale-relevant frontier~\cite{wu2025recent}.

\subsection{Stacking stabilization and morphology engineering}

Layer slippage and exfoliation degrade the columnar $\pi$-channels that
carry charge; stabilizing the stack is therefore a synthesis objective in
its own right. PEG-threading of pores locks the ordered layer arrangement
and preserves HER activity~\cite{zhou2021peg}. Downsizing to colloidal
nano-COFs multiplies mass-normalized rates -- up to
392~mmol\,g$^{-1}$\,h$^{-1}$ -- by shortening exciton diffusion paths and
exposing surface sites~\cite{zhao2024nanoscale}; nanosheet engineering of
$\beta$-ketoamine frameworks with pore-confined sub-nanometer Pt enables
overall water splitting at 0.23\% solar-to-hydrogen efficiency
~\cite{yang2023engineering}. Morphology also mediates composite quality:
in situ growth on oxide scaffolds produces chemically bonded, ultrathin
junction layers~\cite{shen2023in,niu2026fluorine}, and coordination-directed
assembly of MOF-on-COF architectures yields intimate interfacial contact
~\cite{ma2024ii,qi2026construction}. Porphyrin-COF platforms carrying
single-atom metals show that site placement can be encoded synthetically
rather than post-hoc~\cite{he2023porphyrin,chen2021rational}.

The methodological bottom line mirrors the mechanistic one: report the
order parameters (PXRD width, surface area, stacking signature) with the
same prominence as the rate, because the synthesis variables are the
hidden covariates of the entire literature~\cite{qin2024structural,
ghosh2020identification}.
