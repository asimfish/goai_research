\section{Performance Comparison Under Explicit Protocols}
\label{sec:performance}

\subsection{Why raw rates do not rank materials}

Reported HER rates for COF systems span from below
1~mmol\,g$^{-1}$\,h$^{-1}$ to nearly 400~mmol\,g$^{-1}$\,h$^{-1}$, but the
protocol variables -- irradiation spectrum and intensity, sacrificial donor,
cocatalyst identity and loading, catalyst mass, and dispersion state -- move
rates by larger factors than most design levers~\cite{zhong2023oligo,
zhao2024nanoscale,ghosh2020identification}. Mass-normalized rates fall as
loading rises; apparent quantum yield (AQY) at a stated wavelength and
solar-to-hydrogen (STH) efficiency are the only cross-laboratory portable
metrics~\cite{luo2024asymmetric,cheng2023fully}. Pt-size studies underline
the danger of single-variable narratives: across three Tp-series COFs the
photodeposited Pt particle size varied with band gap, yet HER rates showed
no correlation with that size~\cite{wang2026bandgap}. Tables~\ref{tab:single}
and \ref{tab:hetero} therefore list protocol columns explicitly and mark
unreported fields as n.r.; entries are exemplars, not a leaderboard.

\subsection{The cocatalyst-dependence spectrum}

Because Pt cost and scarcity bound any deployment scenario
~\cite{qu2024covalent}, the field is best read as a progression along a
cocatalyst-dependence axis. At the noble-metal end, engineering focuses on
atom economy: single-atom Pt anchored through defined
C$_3$N--Pt--Cl$_2$ coordination on TpPa-1~\cite{dong2021platinum},
heteroatom-tuned Pt single-atom frameworks~\cite{tang2025engineering},
sulfur-chelated high-loading single atoms that remain atom-efficient
(11.4~mmol\,g$^{-1}$\,h$^{-1}$ at 12.1~wt\%)~\cite{chen2024low}, and
low-work-function Pt junctions whose $\Delta G_{\mathrm{H}^*}=-0.13$~eV
optimality yields AQE 80.7\% at 420~nm~\cite{qiu2026atomically}.
Earth-abundant single atoms follow: Co on COFs~\cite{wang2024single},
Fe--Co dual atoms on COF/C$_3$N$_4$~\cite{osemba2026synergistic},
zinc single atoms bridged by copper clusters~\cite{wang2026trinuclear},
spinel cocatalysts with engineered orbital interactions
~\cite{zhang2026electronic}, and DFT screens that rationalize
single-atom--support pairing~\cite{younas2024a,niu2023review}. Molecular
and cluster catalysts occupy the middle ground: cyclometalated Pt in
vinylene frameworks~\cite{jiao2026cyclometalated}, azide-anchored
cobaloximes on COF-42 that outlast their physisorbed analogues
~\cite{gottschling2020rational}, enantioselectively immobilized chiral Co
complexes (5.70~mmol\,g$^{-1}$\,h$^{-1}$ noble-metal-free)
~\cite{huang2025enantioselective}, Cu(II) mediators on chiral
$\beta$-ketoenamine hosts~\cite{weng2022the}, and Ru sites on triazine
sp$^2$c platforms extending to electrocatalytic HER~\cite{zhao2022ruthenium,
yu2024vinyl,zhao2025covalent}. The cocatalyst-free end is growing fastest:
halogen-site TpPaCl~\cite{ma2025engineering}, protonated CdS/COF
architectures~\cite{duan2024protonated}, benzothiazole metal-COFs
~\cite{zhao2025cocatalyst}, JUC-70x@ZIS junctions in seawater
~\cite{zhang2025dual,zhang2025dualx}, black-phosphorus p--n junctions
~\cite{yan2023metal}, and C$_{60}$@TpPa charge-transfer dyads
~\cite{yupeng2026c}.

\subsection{Stability reporting}

Durability claims currently mix hour-scale runs, cycle counts, and
post-mortem characterization. The stronger reports pair retention
percentages with structural verification: 12-hour photocurrent stability
with retained crystallinity~\cite{lu2024copper}, sustained performance of
integrated-interface frameworks~\cite{he2023integrated}, S-scheme junctions
surviving repeated cycling~\cite{dong20222d,zhang2022s,shang20212d,
dong2023fabrication,zhou2025functional,liu2025in}, and functional-group-
resolved junction dynamics~\cite{guo2025engineering,xu2025localized}.
Chirality-induced spin selectivity~\cite{wu2026chiral}, piezo-assisted
charge steering~\cite{gao2025piezoceramics}, and interfacial covalent
bonding~\cite{yi2024constructing,li2025modifying,qi2026construction}
each claim durability side benefits that still await standardized,
long-duration verification -- the protocol gap Section~\ref{sec:challenges}
addresses~\cite{sun2025design,hao2025covalent,doremus2024photocatalytic}.
